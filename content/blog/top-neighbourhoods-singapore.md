# Top 10 Neighbourhoods in Singapore: Where to Live in 2024

Choosing the right neighbourhood in Singapore is just as important as choosing the right property. Each area has its unique character, amenities, and community vibe. Whether you're a young family, working professional, or retiree, this comprehensive guide will help you find the perfect neighbourhood to call home.

## What Makes a Great Neighbourhood?

Before diving into our top 10, here's what we considered:

- **Connectivity:** MRT access, bus services, and proximity to expressways
- **Amenities:** Shopping malls, supermarkets, food options, healthcare
- **Schools:** Quality and variety of educational institutions
- **Community:** Parks, recreational facilities, community centers
- **Safety:** Crime rates and overall security
- **Future Development:** Upcoming projects that add value
- **Property Values:** Price trends and investment potential
- **Lifestyle:** Overall vibe and quality of life

## 1. Bishan - The Family-Friendly Hub

### Why It's Special

Bishan perfectly balances urban convenience with suburban tranquility. Home to Singapore's beloved Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park, this mature estate offers everything families need within walking distance.

### 🚇 **Connectivity: 10/10**

- **MRT:** Bishan MRT (Circle & North-South Lines) - major interchange
- **Buses:** Extensive network with Bishan Bus Interchange
- **Roads:** Easy access to CTE and PIE
- **Travel Time to CBD:** 15-20 minutes

### 🏫 **Schools: 9/10**

**Primary:**

- Ai Tong School
- CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls' School
- Catholic High School (Primary)
- Guangyang Primary

**Secondary:**

- Catholic High School
- Raffles Institution
- CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls' School (Secondary)

**Junior Colleges:**

- Raffles Institution (JC)

### 🏪 **Amenities: 10/10**

**Shopping:**

- Junction 8 (major mall with NTUC, Popular, restaurants)
- Bishan North Shopping Centre
- Shun Li Shopping Centre

**Food:**

- Bishan Street 22 Food Centre (famous!)
- Junction 8 food court and restaurants
- Multiple coffee shops
- McDonald's, KFC, and fast food outlets

**Healthcare:**

- Thomson Medical Centre (15 min drive)
- Numerous GP clinics
- Guardian, Watsons, Unity pharmacies

### 🌳 **Recreation: 10/10**

- **Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park:** Singapore's largest urban park with river, fitness areas, restaurants
- **ActiveSG Gym**
- **Swimming Complex**
- **Multiple playgrounds and fitness corners**
- **MacRitchie Reservoir nearby**

### 💰 **Property Prices (2024)**

**HDB Resale:**

- 3-room: S$450,000-550,000
- 4-room: S$550,000-700,000
- 5-room: S$700,000-900,000

**Condos:**

- Average: S$1,400-1,800 psf
- Premium units: S$1,800-2,200 psf

### ✅ **Pros:**

- Excellent MRT connectivity (2 lines!)
- Family-friendly with great parks
- Mature estate with all amenities
- Good schools
- Central location

### ❌ **Cons:**

- Property prices higher than many areas
- Can be crowded during peak hours
- Limited parking in older HDB blocks

### 👥 **Best For:**

Families with school-age children, professionals who value connectivity, active lifestyle enthusiasts

## How to Choose the Right Neighbourhood for You

### Step 1: Define Your Priorities

**For Families with Children:**

1. Schools (within 1-2km)
2. Parks and playgrounds
3. Safety
4. Community facilities
5. Medical facilities

**For Young Professionals:**

1. Commute time to work
2. MRT accessibility
3. Food and entertainment
4. Gym and fitness
5. Nightlife options

**For Retirees:**

1. Healthcare facilities
2. Peaceful environment
3. Parks for exercise
4. Community centers
5. Accessibility (fewer stairs, lifts)

**For Investors:**

1. Future developments
2. Rental demand
3. Capital appreciation potential
4. Connectivity improvements
5. Property age and remaining lease

### Step 2: Visit at Different Times

Don't just view properties - visit the neighbourhood:

- **Weekday morning:** Check traffic, school crowds
- **Weekday evening:** Test commute, dinner options
- **Weekend afternoon:** See how crowded it gets
- **Late evening:** Check noise levels, safety

### Step 3: Talk to Locals

- Visit coffee shops and chat with residents
- Ask about pros and cons
- Check community Facebook groups
- Speak to security guards

### Step 4: Consider Future Changes

Check:

- **URA Master Plan:** Future developments
- **MRT expansions:** Upcoming stations
- **Government plans:** Town regeneration projects
- **Private developments:** New condos or malls

## Final Thoughts

Choosing a neighbourhood is deeply personal. The "best" neighbourhood for you depends on:

- Your life stage
- Work location
- Budget
- Lifestyle preferences
- Family needs
- Future plans

**Pro Tips:**

1. **Don't just follow trends** - A "popular" area might not suit your needs
2. **Think long-term** - Will this area still work in 5-10 years?
3. **Consider resale potential** - Even if it's your forever home, life changes
4. **Balance heart and head** - Fall in love with a place, but make sure the numbers work
5. **Visit multiple times** - First impressions can be deceiving

Remember, Singapore is compact - even the "far" neighbourhoods are only 30-40 minutes from the CBD. Focus on what makes you happy daily, not just what looks good on paper.
